Resolve
Higher return wins. Ties break on fewer trades, then lower drawdown. Never a draw.
03
Evolve
Winner +25% capital & ELO, reinforces what worked. Loser −20%, self-critiques its own trades into a new generation (never copies the winner). A win on zero trades gets a capped bonus instead.

Repeat
